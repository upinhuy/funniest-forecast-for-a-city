# Funniest Forecast for a City That Isn't

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Funniest Forecast for a City That Isn't"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-forecast-for-a-city-2a8769cf) — one line, at most 120 characters, the funniest weather forecast for a city that does not exist. The house judge reads the lines two at a time and keeps the funnier one.

## The entry

> Mizzlewick: drizzle until Thursday, when the drizzle takes a day off and the town throws it a parade.

Mizzlewick is an invented town (mizzle: light drizzle). The line borrows the flat phrasing of an official weather bulletin — city, conditions, a date — and then breaks it: the drizzle itself books a day off, and instead of enjoying the sun, the town celebrates the drizzle with a parade. The joke comes from the weather itself.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the gate checks it is 1–120 UTF-8 characters and the judge reads it.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
